DARPA Triage Challenge

Developing AI-driven injury detection systems for rapid medical response in mass casualty incidents

Project Overview

During my last semester at CSUCI, I was approached by Dr. Jason Isaacs and asked to join the DARPA Triage Challenge group Coordinated Robotics for Challenge 1 in Perry, Georgia.

The DARPA Triage Challenge's mission is to develop new methods for detecting injury 'signatures' that will help enable medical responders to help patients more quickly, accurately, and at scale. The focus is on mass casualty incidents (MCIs), in both military and civilian contexts, where medical resources are scarce.

Challenge 1 focused on data collection and drone testing. All data recorded was used to train the team's A.I. autonomous models for later challenges and events. This event mimicked a mass casualty event where actors portrayed different wounds and had to act accordingly, ranging from concussions to having missing limbs.

My Roles

I held two primary roles on this team:

Mechatronics Engineer

Helped with the upkeep of our Kepler #67 rover and helped build our Joe #64 rover. Both rovers were equipped with live feed cameras and microphones to record all data in real-time as events unfolded.

Drone Operator

During testing phases at the DARPA site, I remotely controlled the Kepler #67 rover from my computer using a USB-connected controller. Using live feeds from DSLR and infrared cameras, I navigated Kepler to specific areas to record data from actors stationed around the site.

Mission Impact

The DARPA Triage Challenge addresses a critical need in emergency medical response. By developing autonomous systems capable of rapidly detecting and categorizing injuries in mass casualty incidents, this technology has the potential to:

  • Reduce response times for medical personnel in crisis situations
  • Improve accuracy of injury assessment in chaotic environments
  • Scale medical triage capabilities when resources are limited
  • Save lives in both military combat zones and civilian disaster scenarios
